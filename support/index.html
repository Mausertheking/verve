Verve — football, from zero

English

Verve teaches girls and women in Azerbaijan to play football from zero, in Azerbaijani, Russian and English. It works fully offline.

Questions, ideas, mistakes

Found a mistake in a lesson, a translation that sounds wrong, or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/Mausertheking/verve/issues — no account details from the app are ever needed.

Common questions

Do I need the internet? No. Everything, including the videos, is inside the app.

How do I change the language? On the Learn screen, tap AZ · RU · EN at the top.

How do I start over? Tap ⓘ on the Learn screen → Reset my progress.

Where do the rules come from? From the IFAB Laws of the Game 2026/27, explained in plain words.
